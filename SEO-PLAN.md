# KVP Sons — SEO plan for Ernakulam & Kerala

Goal: appear at the top of Google for construction / civil-contractor searches in
Ernakulam (main) and Kerala.

## 1. Target keywords

Exact monthly search numbers need Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads
account; set location to Ernakulam / Kerala). The list below is built from what
currently ranks in Google for this industry and region.

### Main keywords (highest priority — on the page title, H1 and description)
| Keyword | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| construction company in Ernakulam | Main local search for this business |
| civil contractors in Ernakulam | Closest match to what KVP actually does |
| construction company in Kochi | Kochi is how most people name the city |
| civil contractors in Kerala | State-level search |
| Class A government contractor Kerala | Tender/department searches — low competition, high value |
| PWD contractor Kerala | Same — government buyers and partners |

### Service keywords (used in the services section)
- road construction company Kerala / road contractors Ernakulam
- irrigation contractors Kerala, canal construction, dam construction Kerala
- building contractors Ernakulam / Kochi
- PMGSY road contractor, jetty construction Kerala

### Town keywords (used in About + footer + structured data)
Ernakulam · Kochi · Aluva · Muvattupuzha · Kolenchery · Perumbavoor · Kakkanad

### Brand keywords (easy wins — should rank #1 once Google indexes the site)
KVP Sons · K V Paulose and Sons · KV Paulose & Sons Pvt Ltd · Kunnel family contractors

Note: searches like "builders in Kochi" mostly mean apartment/villa builders
(residential real estate). They are a different audience, so they are not targeted.

## 2. What was changed on the website
- Page title: "KVP Sons | Civil Contractors & Construction Company in Ernakulam, Kerala"
- Meta description with Class A, Ernakulam, Kochi, roads, irrigation, PWD
- The page's H1 is now "Civil Contractors in Ernakulam, Kerala · Since 1920"
  (the big "We Build the Foundations of Tomorrow" slogan looks the same)
- Service headings rewritten with keywords (Road Construction, Irrigation, Dam & Canal Works, Building Construction…)
- New lines naming Ernakulam towns in About and the footer
- Structured data (schema.org GeneralContractor): name, legal name, founded 1920,
  founder, Ernakulam, Kerala, areas served, services — helps Google show the business correctly
- Social-share tags (WhatsApp / Facebook previews), canonical link, robots.txt, sitemap.xml
- Image descriptions (alt text) for the logo and photos

## 3. What has to happen OFF the website (this is where #1 local rankings come from)
These need the company's own accounts, so the owner (or you) must do them:

1. **Google Business Profile** — the single most important step for "near me" and
   map results in Ernakulam. Create it at business.google.com with the exact office
   address, phone, category "General contractor" (plus "Road construction company",
   "Civil engineering company"), hours, photos of real sites, and the website link.
   Ask past clients and partners for Google reviews.
2. **Google Search Console** — add the site at search.google.com/search-console,
   verify it, and submit `sitemap.xml`. This gets the site indexed quickly.
3. **Own domain** — move from `rohaneldhose2008.github.io/kvps` to a domain such as
   `kvpsons.com` or `kvpaulose.com` (GitHub Pages supports custom domains).
   A proper domain ranks and looks far better.
4. **Same name, address, phone everywhere** — list the business on Justdial,
   IndiaMART, Sulekha, Houzz India and the D&B directory with exactly the same
   details as the Google profile. These directories currently fill the top results
   for Ernakulam contractor searches, so being listed there also brings direct leads.
5. **Put the real contact details on the site** — local ranking relies on the office
   address and phone number being on the website and matching Google.
6. **Real project photos and project pages** — a page per major project
   (e.g. "Kochi Metro works", "Idamalayar irrigation") with location names gives
   Google much more to rank.
7. **Mentions and links** — news articles, PWD/tender listings, Kerala contractor
   associations, and client websites linking to the site.

Rankings take weeks to months to build; no one can guarantee position #1, but the
steps above are what the top results in this region have in common.
